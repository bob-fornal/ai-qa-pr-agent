## Critical Path Details

### CP-1: Checkout total with discount applied (P0, Impact 5 × Likelihood 4 = 20)

- **Entry point(s):** `POST /api/orders/{id}/checkout`
- **What changed:** `OrderPricingService.calculateTotal()` now ignores unapproved discounts > 15%
- **Why it's critical:** a wrong total charges customers incorrectly (financial loss and refunds)
- **Existing coverage:** `OrderPricingService.spec` covers discounts ≤ 15% only
- **Preconditions / test data:** order with line items; discounts at 15%, 15.01%, 30%; approved and unapproved

**Automated** (unit, integration): the logic is deterministic money math with a new boundary, and regressions would be silent.
- Given a 15% discount, total includes the discount with no approval required
- Given a 15.01% unapproved discount, total excludes the discount and the order is flagged `pending_approval`
- Given a 30% approved discount, total includes the discount; rounding matches the currency's minor unit
- Integration: checkout endpoint persists the same total the service computed

**Smoke** (staging, prod): checkout is the primary revenue path.
- Place a small order with a ≤ 15% discount; total is correct and the order completes

**Not recommended:** Manual (covered deterministically by automation); E2E (the integration test plus smoke covers it at lower cost)
