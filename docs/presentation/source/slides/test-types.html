Three kinds of testing, three different jobs

Automated

Repeatable checks in CI: unit, integration, contract, E2E.

Use for: logic, boundaries, contracts, role matrices, every bug fix.

Smoke

Fast pass/fail checks after each deploy. Five minutes or less.

Use for: config, flags, migrations, upgrades, integrations, P0 paths.

Manual

A person runs scripted cases or exploratory charters.

Use for: UX, accessibility, new features, third parties with no sandbox.

Each type is optional, but every critical path gets at least one. Every choice, and every omission, carries a reason tied to the path's risk.
