## Before You Start

1. Check out `feature/discount-approval` (head `8687dd2`) and `cd sample-app`.
   - Run `node -v` and confirm it's 20 or higher: `db.js` now uses `toSorted()`.
   - There's no `npm install`: the app has no dependencies.
   - The data is in memory (`DATA_FILE` defaults to `:memory:`), so every restart resets orders 1001, 1002 and 1003 to their seed values.
   - The commands below use bash syntax. In PowerShell, use `$env:NAME='value'; npm start`, and clear variables between runs with `Remove-Item Env:NAME`, because they stay set for the session.
2. Run `npm test`. Expected: 0 failures and exactly 1 skipped test, "tax rounds to the nearest cent" in `test/pricing.test.js` (marked "TODO: re-enable after the cents migration settles"). Any failure, or more than one skip, stops the checklist here.
3. Run `FEATURE_DISCOUNT_APPROVAL=true npm start` and open http://localhost:3000 with the browser dev tools open on the Network tab. The demo users are sam (sales), maria (manager) and ada (admin). To switch user, pick a name in "Sign in as" and click Sign in.

### D-1: App boots and health reports the flag (CP-5, P0)

1. With the app started as in Before You Start, run `curl http://localhost:3000/health`.
2. Open http://localhost:3000, pick sam and click Sign in.

**Expected:** the response body is exactly `{"status":"ok","features":{"discountApproval":true}}`, and the "Order ID" form appears after sign-in.
**If it fails:** Node is older than 20 (`toSorted()` in `src/db.js`), or check the flag parsing in `src/config.js` (only the lowercase string `true` enables the flag).

### D-2: Seed orders show correct cent-based totals (CP-1, P0; also CP-7)

1. As sam, load order 1001.
2. Load order 1002, then order 1003.
3. In the Network tab, open the response of `GET /api/orders/1001`.

**Expected:**
- 1001: Subtotal $45.00, Discount $0.00 (0%), Tax $3.15, Total $48.15.
- 1002: $45.00, $4.50 (10%), $2.84, $43.34.
- 1003: status `paid`, $30.15, $0.00 (0%), $2.11, $32.26.
- The JSON has `subtotalCents: 4500`, `taxCents: 315`, `totalCents: 4815` and `discountStatus: "none"`, and no `subtotal`, `discount`, `tax` or `total` keys.

**If it fails:** look at `calculateTotal` in `src/pricing.js` (`toCents` or rounding), or `money()` in `public/app.js` not dividing by 100. If 1003's tax isn't $2.11, that's the bug the skipped rounding test should catch.

### D-3: 30% discount waits for approval; manager approves (CP-2, P0; also CP-10)

1. As sam, load order 1001, set Discount % to 30 and click Apply discount.
2. Sign in as maria, load order 1001 and click Approve in the banner.
3. In the Network tab, check the response of the review call.

**Expected:**
- After step 1, the banner "Discount pending manager approval" is visible, with Discount $0.00 (30%) and Total $48.15.
- After step 2, the banner is hidden, with Discount $13.50 (30%), Tax $2.21 and Total $33.71.
- The review response has `approvalStatus: "approved"` and `approvedBy: "maria"`.

**If it fails:** look at `setDiscount` / `reviewDiscount` in `src/orders.js`, `totalsFor` passing `approved`, or the banner toggle in `render()` in `public/app.js`.

### D-4: Threshold boundary and reject (CP-2, P0)

1. As sam, load order 1002, set 15 and click Apply discount.
2. Set 16 and click Apply discount.
3. Sign in as maria, load order 1002 and click Reject.

**Expected:**
- 15% applies immediately: no banner, Discount $6.75 (15%), Tax $2.68, Total $40.93.
- 16% shows the banner, with Total $48.15.
- After Reject, the banner is hidden, with Discount $0.00 (0%) and Total $48.15.

**If it fails:** check whether the threshold comparison should be `>` or `>=`. It's duplicated in `src/orders.js` and `src/pricing.js`. Also check the reject patch in `reviewDiscount`.

### D-5: Sales can't approve; admin can (CP-3, P0)

1. As sam, load order 1002, set 40 and click Apply discount. The banner shows.
2. Still as sam, click Approve.
3. Load order 1002 again.
4. Sign in as ada, load order 1002 and click Approve.

**Expected:**
- Step 2 shows "Only managers can review discounts" (HTTP 403 in the Network tab).
- After step 3, the banner still shows and the Total is $48.15.
- After step 4, the banner is hidden, with Discount $18.00 (40%), Tax $1.89 and Total $28.89.

**If it fails:** look at the review route in `src/server.js`. It uses the deny-list `user.role === 'sales'` instead of `hasRole(user, ['manager', 'admin'])`, so watch for any role other than sales getting through. Sales users can also see the Approve and Reject buttons.

### D-6: Checkout while pending; review after paid (CP-2, P0)

1. As sam, load order 1001 (approved at 30% in D-3), set 40 and click Apply discount. The banner should reappear, with Total $48.15.
2. Click Checkout.
3. Sign in as maria, load order 1001 and click Approve.

**Expected:**
- Step 1 puts the order back to pending (banner shown, `approvedBy: null` in the response).
- Step 2 is refused with an error message, and the status stays `open`.
- Step 3 is refused with an error message, and the Total doesn't change.

**Likely fails today:**
- `checkout()` doesn't check `approvalStatus`, so the order becomes `paid` at $48.15.
- `reviewDiscount()` doesn't check `order.status`, so the approval succeeds. The paid order then displays $28.89 while `paidTotalCents` is 4815.

The intended behavior needs confirmation from the PO.

**If it fails:** look at `checkout` and `reviewDiscount` in `src/orders.js`.

**If it fails:** look at `migrations/002_discount_approval.js` (the `Math.round(paidTotal * 100)` conversion) or `runMigrations` in `src/db.js`.

### D-8: Invalid threshold doesn't silently disable approval (CP-6, P0)

## Left for QA

- **CP-1 Order pricing and checkout totals in cents:** needs finance sign-off on one-cent rounding differences against the previous release, using real orders.
- **CP-2 Large-discount approval lifecycle:** needs concurrent reviews from two sessions, a decision on the self-approval policy, and a staging smoke run with the flag on.
- **CP-3 Authorization to review discounts:** an unlisted role or a tampered token needs test-signed tokens. That belongs in the automated role matrix (QA item A-5), not a manual check.
- **CP-4 Migration 002 on persisted order data:** needs a staging copy of production data, reconciliation of paid totals, and a restore rehearsal. There's no `down` migration.
- **CP-5 Service startup on the Node 22 container:** needs the `node:22-alpine` image build, the `HEALTHCHECK` running under orchestration (it hardcodes port 3000, but the app honors `PORT`), and a CI matrix for Node 20/22.
- **CP-6 Approval flag and threshold configuration:** needs per-environment flag values, and toggling the flag on staging while live orders are pending.
- **CP-7 Order API response contract (dollars → cents):** it's unknown whether anything besides `public/app.js` uses the removed dollar fields.
- **CP-8 Manager webhook notification and PII:** needs a real webhook receiver in staging and a privacy review of the log tooling.
- **CP-9 Admin CSV order export:** needs the export opened in Excel and Sheets with hostile data (commas, quotes, a leading `=`) to check for formula injection.
- **CP-10 Order screen (cents display and approval banner):** needs a browser matrix, a contrast check of the banner (`#a3a3a3` on `#fef9c3`), and screen-reader and keyboard checks.

## Handoff Notes for QA

**Env vars and flags QA must set:**
- `FEATURE_DISCOUNT_APPROVAL=true`. Only the exact lowercase string turns it on; the Dockerfile defaults it to `false`.
- `DISCOUNT_APPROVAL_THRESHOLD` defaults to 15. A non-numeric value disables approval (see D-8).
- `NOTIFIER_WEBHOOK_URL`.
- `DATA_FILE`, for any testing with persisted data or migrations.

**Accounts and data used:**
- Users: sam (sales), maria (manager), ada (admin).
- Seed orders: 1001 (open, 0%), 1002 (open, 10%), 1003 (paid; CAP at $10.05 × 3).
- In-memory data resets on every restart. D-7 uses `C:/temp/pr1-legacy.json`, created by running `main`.

**Known issues these checks are expected to surface:**
- The tax-rounding regression test is `test.skip`'d, even though the PR claims a rounding fix.
- Checkout is allowed while a discount is pending, and reviews are accepted on paid orders (D-6).
- A `NaN` threshold silently disables approval (D-8).
- The customer email is logged and posted to an unsigned webhook (D-9).
- The review route uses a role deny-list instead of `hasRole`, and sales users see the Approve and Reject buttons.
- The CSV export doesn't escape values, and recalculates totals instead of using `paidTotalCents`.
- `/health` exposes the feature flags without authentication.

**Open questions for the PR author or PO:**
- Should checkout be blocked while a discount is pending, and should reviews be refused on orders that aren't open?
- May managers or admins approve their own discount requests?
- What should happen to existing over-threshold orders when the flag is turned on, and to pending orders when it's turned off?
- Should the export show `paidTotalCents` or the recalculated total?
- Is logging and sending `customerEmail` approved? Who owns the webhook receiver?
- Why is the rounding test skipped if this PR fixes rounding?
- Has dropping Node 18 been agreed? Which Node version does CI use?
